# Archive: drafting material of the first release (July 2026)

`sections/` and `notes/` are the section drafts and design notes written for the
first release (v0.1.0, commit `a82f935`). They are kept for history and are **not
maintained**. Numbers, claims and even some mechanisms described here are
superseded: for example they predate the centred amplitude, the calibrated
significance test, the level spent over repeated read-outs, experiment E5, the
time-based windows and the node diagnostics, and they quote a Periodic
false-positive rate of 1/5 that the corrected engine no longer has.

Read the manuscript instead: [`../arxiv_ref/`](../arxiv_ref/) (PDF
[`../strata_paper.pdf`](../strata_paper.pdf)).
