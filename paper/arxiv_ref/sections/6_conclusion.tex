\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:concl}

\sname\ decides between durable and plastic with a sequential probability ratio test whose two hit rates and two error rates the user states. We showed that the OctoMap increments of the first release are such a test between the hit rates \SqLegMzero{} and \SqLegMone{}, with hypotheses that were not chosen, and that a multiplicative leak removes the property on which its error bound rests. Engine version 0.2 derives increments and thresholds from $(m_0,m_1,\alpha,\beta)$ and ends every cell Static, Rejected or Undecided. Under independent windows clutter at a hit rate of at most $m_0$ graduates in the first test of one cell history (up to its first graduation, its first rejection or its retention as a periodic candidate) with probability at most \SqVille{}, and restarts after a demotion are not covered; the operating characteristic of the engine with the Periodic veto off matches the exact computation of the rule and is approximated by Wald's closed form; and at lag-1 autocorrelations of $0.23$ and $0.5$ the graduation rate per forward test (a history split at each demotion) is \SqAcTw{} and \SqAcHalf{} times $\alpha$, while real calibration cells lie between \CalLagLo{} and \CalLagHi{}, so we state no guarantee for real windows. The pre-registered calibration, amended after its first measurement, did not freeze: the wall-thickness gate failed on every calibration sequence, the shipped pair $(0.20,0.70,0.01,0.01)$ is provisional, and the 3D pair is not calibrated. On held-out LiDAR data from LILocBench, Oxford Spires, the KTH benchmark and Boreas, seven evidence rules differ in direction on different measures, and we pick none. On LILocBench clutter the realised per-cell rate of the sequential test is \PtLilAllSprt\,\% (\PtLilLeSprt\,\% at an observed hit rate of at most $m_0$) against \PtLilAllShipped\,\% for the rule of the first release; both are above the nominal per-history $\alpha=0.01$. The same per-cell state machine serves a 2D grid and a 3D voxel hash and builds without ROS under 114 behaviour-level tests. Its FreMEn-style periodic label has a false-alarm bound for iid clutter; on held-out synthetic seeds it labels at most \SqEfiveLegWorst{} (legacy arm) and \SqEfiveWorst{} (sprt arm) of \SqCellsAperiodic{} Bernoulli clutter cells Periodic at some window within \SqEfiveHorizon{} windows, and it is validated on synthetic streams only. \sname\ is offered as a small module whose defaults are provisional: re-derive the pair for your sensor, your window length and your resolution, and read the realised rates beside the nominal ones.
