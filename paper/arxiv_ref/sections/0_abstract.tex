\begin{abstract}
A robot that maps one building for weeks must keep walls, forget movers and treat a scheduled door as signal. Occupancy mappers make the first two decisions with a log-odds accumulator whose increments were chosen by habit. We show that the OctoMap increments ($+0.85$, $-0.4$) are the increments of Wald's sequential probability ratio test (SPRT) between the hit rates \SqLegMzero{} and \SqLegMone{}, so that ``Static'' means ``occupied in more than about a third of the windows'', and that a multiplicative leak removes the property on which an error bound rests. \sname\ 0.2 makes the test explicit: the user states two hit rates and two error rates, increments and thresholds follow, and a cell ends Static, Rejected or Undecided. Under independent windows, clutter at a hit rate of at most $m_0$ is declared Static in one cell history with probability at most \SqVille\ (Ville's inequality), and the operating characteristic measured on the engine matches the exact computation of the rule and is approximated by Wald's closed form; at a lag-1 autocorrelation of $0.23$ the per-history rate is \SqAcTw{} times the bound. We derived the pair under a pre-registered rule, but the wall-thickness gate failed on every calibration sequence, so the shipped pair $(0.20,0.70,0.01,0.01)$ is provisional and the 3D pair is not calibrated. On held-out LILocBench, Oxford Spires, KTH-benchmark and Boreas data we compare seven evidence rules, without tuning and without choosing a winner. On LILocBench clutter, \PtLilAllSprt\,\% of the cells are ever Static under the sequential test (\PtLilLeSprt\,\% at an observed hit rate of at most $m_0$) against \PtLilAllShipped\,\% under v0.1; both exceed the nominal $\alpha=0.01$, which is per history, not per cell. The sequential test rejects none of the \PtWallN{} wall-run cells and needs a median of \PtWallMedSprt{} windows to graduate one, against \PtWallMedShipped{}. The engine is one geometry-free state machine for a 2D grid and a 3D voxel hash, with a FreMEn-style periodic label whose false-alarm bound holds for iid clutter; Periodic is validated on synthetic streams only. Code: \url{https://github.com/kjungmo/strata}.
\end{abstract}
